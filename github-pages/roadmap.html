PRODUCT ROADMAP

Make every room more ready.

The roadmap keeps LessonCue local-first and easy to operate while strengthening live playback, planning, accessibility, security, recovery, and large-site administration.

Priority 11, immediate signage reliability and screen-purpose controls, is implemented and validated. The comprehensive checkbox roadmap remains the source of truth; Priority 12 is now the next planned milestone.
COMPLETE · PRIORITY 11

Reliable signage publishing and purpose-built screens

Save, publish, and push signage with visible progress and actionable errors; place QR codes with horizontal labels; format every standard text-bearing element; use approved iCalendar feeds; and manage signage-only, temporary, or permanent browser displays directly.

NEXT · PRIORITY 12

Media upload and library workflow

Add resumable pause-and-retry transfers, folder drag-and-drop, stronger progress and duplicate handling, favorites and collections, improved search, safe reference consolidation, and administrator-defined upload and storage limits.

SHIPPED · v0.29

Validated Intel Quick Sync acceleration

Use compatible Intel graphics for universal and adaptive H.264 conversion, verify every result, report the selected engine, and retry safely in software whenever hardware fails.

SHIPPED · v0.28

Registration and account self-service

Choose closed, code-required, or open registration; verify email; recover passwords; edit profiles; manage registration codes; and deliver account messages through a locally protected Resend or Brevo configuration.

SHIPPED · v0.27

Full-screen browser and projector playback

Pair a Windows, Mac, Linux, or ChromeOS browser like a TV, then run local media, online cues, pre-roll, countdowns, and lessons with phone, keyboard, or presentation-remote control.

SHIPPED · v0.26

Safe Android TV self-updates

Check, download, verify, and install signed LessonCue TV releases from the remote—with quiet daily checks, manual controls, trusted-host and checksum enforcement, and Android-owned confirmation.

SHIPPED · v0.24

Consistent media organization

Define approved hierarchical folders and tags once, then give every uploader clear choices in lesson and Media Library workflows.

SHIPPED · v0.22

Edit safely and recover deletions

Edit or remove classrooms and recover deleted classrooms, lessons, and media for 30 days from an administrator-only recycling bin.

SHIPPED · v0.21

Dedicated classroom controllers

Give each room a branded path or hostname, room/lesson QR launch, expiring restricted sessions, and a saved-to-phone controller while retaining a PIN-protected universal remote.

SHIPPED · v0.20

Optional secure remote access

Connect an administrator-owned hostname through a restricted, outbound-only Cloudflare Tunnel while keeping local-only operation as the default.

SHIPPED · v0.19

Bulk planning and media operations

Upload multiple files and select lessons, playlist cues, or media to rename, move, shift, archive, organize, retain, or remove them together.

SHIPPED · v0.18

Actionable screen diagnostics

Inspect cache files, queued downloads, decoders, recent errors, network quality, and clock drift—with privacy-gated one-time screenshots.

SHIPPED · v0.12–0.17

Edit, reuse, and organize

Visual timelines, media folders and versions, presentation conversion, recurring templates, restore, reliable TV video, and granular permissions are complete.
